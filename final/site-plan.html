mStore Site Plan

Emmanuel Onukwue Project Site Plan

Site Name

mStore — Online Product Showcase

The name mStore was selected because it is short, memorable, and clearly communicates an online store or product showcase. The "m" suggests "modern," "market," or simply a brandable prefix, while "Store" immediately tells visitors what the site is about. The name is easy to type and works well as a domain (e.g., mstore.com).

Site Purpose

mStore is a dynamic product showcase website that allows visitors to browse a curated collection of products fetched from the Fake Store API. The site provides:

  • A landing page featuring featured products and site navigation.
  • A products page that dynamically displays at least 15 products with details such as title, price, category, and rating.
  • A contact page with a form that captures user inquiries and displays submitted data on a form action page.
  • Interactive features including modal dialogs for product details, local storage for user preferences, and responsive design for all devices.

Scenarios

These represent questions from the target audience that will drive the site's content:

  1. Scenario 1: "I want to quickly browse a variety of products and see their prices and ratings without having to visit multiple pages. Can I do that here?"
  2. Scenario 2: "I found a product I'm interested in. How can I view more details about it and save my favorite products for later?"
  3. Scenario 3: "I have a question about a product or want to send feedback. Is there a way to contact the store directly?"

Color Scheme

The following colors were selected to create a clean, modern, and trustworthy look:

Color Hex Code Usage
Deep Indigo #1B2A4A Header background, footer background, primary headings
Amber Gold #F2A900 Buttons, accents, hover states, star ratings
Off-White #F8F9FA Page background, card backgrounds
Charcoal #2D2D2D Body text, paragraphs

This palette uses the design principles of contrast (dark indigo against off-white), repetition (consistent use of amber for interactive elements), and alignment (clean, grid-based layout).

Typography

Two Google Fonts are used to create a clean and readable hierarchy:

Font Usage Example
Poppins (sans-serif) Headings, navigation, buttons Featured Product
Open Sans (sans-serif) Body text, paragraphs, form labels Browse our collection of products.

Poppins provides a modern, geometric look for headings, while Open Sans offers excellent readability for body content. Both are loaded via Google Fonts.
